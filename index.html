SMALL STEPS, BETTER HEALTH

Everyday Healthy Living

Simple habits can support physical health, mental wellbeing and community health.

🥗

Eat Well

Aim for a varied, balanced diet that includes vegetables, fruits, whole grains and suitable sources of protein.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink safe water regularly and adjust your intake to your activity level, weather and individual needs.

🏃

Keep Moving

Include suitable physical activity in your routine and reduce long periods of sitting.

😴

Rest Well

Maintain a regular sleep routine and allow your body time to recover.

🧼

Practise Hygiene

Wash your hands appropriately, handle food safely and maintain a clean environment.

🤝

Support Wellbeing

Build supportive relationships, manage stress and seek help when you need it.
